    mediasync: CB2 fix video is stutter when starting playback [1/1]
    
    PD#SWPL-94587
    
    Problem:
    do timeshift,video is stutter when starting playback.
    After entering the time shift,
    it will pause. After a period of time, after the resume,
    because the audio does not start, the RefClock is not updated.
    The pcr is much larger than the pts, and the video will fast forward.
    
    Solution:
    After resume, RefClock is updated if the difference between pcr
    and first pts is greater than the cache threshold.
    
    Verify:
    SC2
