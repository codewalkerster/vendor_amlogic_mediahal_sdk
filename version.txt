videodec: CB2 avi file played stuck [2/2]

PD#OTT-59741

Problem:
The PTS of AVI files does not increase continuously and uniformly. We
cannot simply use the FLAG of UNSTABLE_PTS to incrementally calculate
the PTS.
Because most videos in AVI contain DiscardFlag, this will cause some
frames to need to display multiple FrameDurations.

Solution:
Set the new Flag to codec2 and use the new logic to calculate the PTS of
the AVI video.

Verify:
Android U
