    tsplayer: CB1 FCC, switching channel video freezes.  [1/1]
    
    PD#SWPL-64409
    
    Problem:
    1. Because the rend queue is not cleared in time,
    in the current normal mode,
    there is a certain probability that the slot of the
    previous normal mode will be sent to the surface queue,
    and the surface queue is abnormal, causing the video to freeze.
    2. At the moment when VideodecNonTunnelWrapper is
    still onOutputFormatChanged() applying for buffer allocation,
    setworkmode (cache mode) is called.
    At this time, when videodec is just allocated to
    a part of the buffer in the queue,
    because it is set to cache mode, the buffer just applied for is cleared again.
    
    Solution:
    1. The queue is uniformly time stamped,
    and only the slot applied for in the current normal mode is used,
    and if it is not, it is discarded.
    2. The setworkmode and onOutputFormatChanged() of VideodecNonTunnelWrapper
    are locked to prevent abnormal application buffer process.
    
    Verify:
    SC2
    
    Change-Id: I38c60c64c504fcce970622caf7b1292739c83a4a
    Signed-off-by: yunmin.chen <yunmin.chen@amlogic.com>
